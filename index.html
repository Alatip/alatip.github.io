Where the Swarm Pays

One corpus of answer traces, seven reasoning-model families, 240 hard problems, every cheap way of multiplying agents. On the answer nothing leaves the box between the best member and the "someone was right" ceiling: plurality is below the best model on every layer, debate fixes 1 of 12 unanimously wrong problems and half of its gain is re-reading one's own answer, personas add no independence, synthesis recovers nothing. On verification the swarm pays: five weak judges out-pick the best judge and the solvers' plurality (0.867 vs 0.82 vs 0.80) because their picking errors barely correlate, and peer disagreement flags errors at AUROC 0.92 against 0.82 for self-resampling. Asked to certify a single candidate they are solvers again. Measured, not pre-registered; ≈ $50 of inference; dataset to be released within a month. doi

Debate Is Not Sampling

The modal ceiling of test-time scaling says plurality voting cannot beat the rate at which the most common answer is right. On 9,600 public debate traces, three-agent refinement beats the round-0 modal-hit rate on every weak model and corrects 31–45% of problems where all three agents started wrong; the exchangeability premise is what fails. The correlation ceiling does transfer: inter-agent correlation rises every round and the panel is one effective voter by round 3; stopping at unanimity beats the n_eff-plateau rule. Pre-registered, zero inference cost. code · doi

The Control Ate the Signal

Kim 2026 finds that diversity metrics barely predict majority-vote gain once accuracy is controlled for. On four public correctness matrices a heterogeneous Condorcet model with no dependence term predicts the same gain at Spearman 0.80 against 0.20 for the residualized metric; the tetrachoric dependence estimate adds 0.01. Gain over the best member is a nonlinear function of the accuracies that a linear control throws away. Pre-registered, zero inference cost. code · doi

There Is No Free Lunch After All

Notes before writing down a theorem. The boundary of what a model can solve is the closure of its training set under composition of bounded depth; swarms, chains, debate and short RLVR move mass inside it, only external data extends it. The claim, why it is nearly a tautology, where the content is, what threatens it, and the plan to test it. Nothing measured yet. doi
